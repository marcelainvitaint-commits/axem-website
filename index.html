FAQ

Questions we hear often

How is your approach different from a typical agency?
+−

We start by understanding exactly who your dream client is, what they need, and how your offer fits. Before we contact anyone, we identify companies that are actively facing the problem you solve. We track signals like new hires, funding rounds, team changes, and company updates to find prospects at the right moment. This way every message goes to someone who is genuinely interested in what you offer.

How do you actually reach out to potential clients?
+−

We use a combination of email and LinkedIn to connect with decision-makers at the right companies. Everything is built around your ideal client profile and the signals we track. We set up and manage the full technical infrastructure so messages land where they should, including LinkedIn profile optimisation. From there we write, test, and improve every message based on what is getting replies and what is not.

How do you reach more people without losing the personal touch?
+−

Most companies only scratch the surface of their market because manual research and outreach takes too long. There are far more potential clients out there than most teams ever reach. We use AI and automation to cover more ground, faster, while keeping every message relevant and personal. AI does not replace the human touch, it amplifies it. The result is a wider reach without losing the quality of the conversation.

Will AI outreach sound robotic?
+−

No. AI helps us gather better data, but we combine technology and human copywriting experts to create personalised, effective messages that add value, feel natural and build trust.

How do you help close more contracts once leads are coming in?
+−

We use AI and automation once we understand your flow to fill in the gaps where we detect you are losing leads and contracts. This usually means faster first responses when a lead shows interest, higher show-up rates, follow-ups so no lead goes cold, quicker proposals, a briefing before every call, all your lead information in one place, and clear numbers to know what is working and what to improve.

Do I still need an SDR team?
+−

You can have SDRs, but with our system, you don't have to hire and train more people to see results. We help you grow your pipeline without the delays and extra expenses of building a team, so you can focus on closing deals.

What if our product is niche or technical?
+−

We take the time to understand your market, your ideal clients, and the specific language they use. This helps us create messages that feel written by a domain expert, with value and that speak directly to their needs.
